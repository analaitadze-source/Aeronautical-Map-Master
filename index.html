Chart Reference Status

Deck status: The deck contains 125 Annex 4 Appendix 2 chart symbols, including 25 aerodrome/heliport and obstacle-chart symbols, and 25 Annex 14 Volume I physical airport markings, signs and lights. The Annex 14 cards describe physical aerodrome surfaces and equipment; they are not chart symbols. The accessible FOCA Annex 4 copy is marked as an in-progress consolidation with an amendment record indicating Amendment 62. ICAO's catalogue lists Amendments 57–63, but Amendment 63 text was not independently checked. Annex 14 Volume I is listed as the 9th Edition (July 2022), including Amendment 18; Corrigenda 1–3 are separately listed, with Corrigendum 3 dated 8 June 2026. Their texts were not checked clause-by-clause. The full Doc 8697 text was not independently inspected, so current consolidated compliance is not claimed.

Card Check: Choose the correct definition for one symbol from two options. Each answer earns one mark; shuffle or continue to check another card before moving to the quiz.

Quiz: Filter the three aerodrome groups by difficulty: Easy uses one group, Medium mixes two, and Hard uses all three. Choose identification or multiple matching, a question/pair count, and Version A, B, or C. Each correct identification or pair earns one mark; Start / Shuffle Quiz creates a shuffled round.

Artwork: Card SVGs are study redrawings of the cited symbol plates, not approved chart artwork. Check the applicable chart and legend; do not use these cards for operational navigation or chart production.
