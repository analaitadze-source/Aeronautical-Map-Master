# Aeronautical Map Master

The deck contains 125 curated ICAO Annex 4 Appendix 2 chart symbols and 25 ICAO Annex 14 Volume I physical airport markings, signs, and lights. The Annex 4 set includes 25 aerodrome/heliport and obstacle-chart symbols (Appendix 2, APP 2-20); the Annex 14 set depicts physical airport features, not chart symbols. The selection is not statistically ranked by frequency. Each card displays its source, applicable standard section, version notes, and compliance limitations.

The accessible FOCA-hosted Annex 4 copy is the 11th Edition (July 2009). Its amendment record indicates Amendment 62, while the file label marks the consolidation as in progress. ICAO's official listing includes Amendments 57–63, but Amendment 63 text was not independently inspected. The deck therefore does not claim current consolidated Annex 4 compliance.

ICAO Annex 14, Volume I is listed as the 9th Edition (July 2022), including Amendment 18. Corrigenda 1–3 are separately listed; Corrigendum 3 is dated 8 June 2026. Their texts were not checked clause-by-clause, so the Annex 14 cards are study redrawings, not a current-compliance determination. ICAO Doc 8697 is listed as the 3rd Edition (2016), with Corrigendum 1 and Amendment 1 dated 11 December 2020. Its full text was not independently inspected, so no provision-level Doc 8697 compliance claim is made. These flashcards are for study only, not for operational use, flight planning, chart production, or aerodrome certification.

Colors on the Annex 4 cards follow the applicable Appendix 2 plate, including exceptions such as black spot-elevation text and yellow caution panels. The new aerodrome/heliport chart symbols are monochrome black as shown on the accessible Appendix 2 plate. Annex 14 cards use the prescribed color conventions for runway/taxiway markings, signs, and lighting. All SVGs are simplified study artwork, not dimensional or approved diagrams; refer to the current ICAO publication, national AIP, and applicable chart legend before operational use.

The quiz supports identification and multi-pair matching. Easy quizzes use one aerodrome group, Medium mixes two, and Hard uses all three; each mode offers configurable lengths and Versions A–C. Questions and choices are shuffled, and each correct answer or pair earns one mark.
